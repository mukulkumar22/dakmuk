The hidden place

Orchha

Orchha, meaning “the hidden place”, was founded in 1531 by the Bundela king Rudra Pratap Singh on the banks of the Betwa. For two centuries it was the capital of the Bundela kings, who filled it with palaces, temples and riverside chhatris that still stand almost untouched.

Today it is a quiet town set in forest along the river. It is also the one place where Lord Ram is worshipped as a king: at the Ram Raja temple he receives a daily guard of honour from the police.
