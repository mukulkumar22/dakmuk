Jhansi station Amar Mahal 16 km · 30 min Betwa
Open in Maps

By train

Get off at Jhansi (VGLJ, earlier Jhansi Junction / JHS), about 30 minutes by road from Orchha. Suggested trains from Delhi (about 4½ hours):

  • Gatimaan Express: from Hazrat Nizamuddin
  • Bhopal Shatabdi: from New Delhi

Plenty of other trains run to Jhansi too. Please check times on IRCTC before booking.

By air

The nearest airports are Gwalior (about 2½ hours by road) and Khajuraho (about 3½ hours).
